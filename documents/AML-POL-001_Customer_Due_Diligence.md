# AML-POL-001: Customer Due Diligence (CDD) and Customer Identification Program (CIP) Standard

**Document ID:** AML-POL-001  
**Version:** v3.2  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-15  
**Classification:** Internal Compliance Policy  
**Target Audience:** FIU Investigators, Compliance Officers, Onboarding Analysts  

---

## 1. Overview and Purpose
This document establishes the mandatory standards for verifying customer identities and assessing money-laundering risk at onboarding and throughout the customer lifecycle. Compliance with this standard is required under Bank Secrecy Act (BSA) and USA PATRIOT Act regulations.

## 2. Customer Identification Requirements (CIP)
Every customer opening an account must provide verifiable identity data before account activation:
- **Natural Persons:** Full legal name, date of birth, residential address, and government identification number (SSN, ITIN, or Passport number with country of issuance).
- **Legal Entities:** Certificate of incorporation, Employer Identification Number (EIN), registered corporate address, and identification of all Ultimate Beneficial Owners (UBOs) holding 25% or greater equity interest.

## 3. Risk Categorization Tiers
Customers are categorized into three distinct risk tiers:
1. **Low Risk:** Domestic retail customers with standard salary deposits and no foreign remittances.
2. **Medium Risk:** Commercial enterprises operating in non-cash-intensive domestic sectors.
3. **High Risk:** Non-resident aliens, Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs), cash-intensive businesses (CIBs), foreign correspondent accounts, and virtual asset service providers (VASPs).

## 4. Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) Trigger Rules
Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) must be initiated within 48 business hours when any of the following triggers occur:
- Initial customer risk score exceeds 75/100.
- Customer is identified as a PEP or immediate family member of a PEP.
- High-value wire transfers originate from or are directed to FATF-designated high-risk jurisdictions.
- Adverse media screening produces confirmed matches related to financial crime, fraud, bribery, or tax evasion.

EDD packages require validation of the legitimate source of wealth (SOW), source of funds (SOF), and formal sign-off by a Level-2 Senior AML Compliance Manager.
