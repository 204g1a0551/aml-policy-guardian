AML-GUIDE-004: Transaction Escalation Guidelines and Decision Matrix
Version: v2.8
Classification: Compliance Operating Guideline
Effective Date: 2026-02-15
Owner: Office of the Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO)

Section 1: Objective and Governance
These guidelines provide a formalized decision framework for escalating suspicious transactions, high-risk alerts, and emergent financial crime situations to senior compliance leadership, the MLRO, and executive committees.

Section 2: Four-Tier Escalation Hierarchy
1. Tier 1 - Alert Ingestion & L1 Review: Handled by AML Operations Analysts. Focuses on clearing routine false positives and triaging complex behaviors.
2. Tier 2 - Senior Case Investigation: Handled by Senior FIU Investigators. Conducts in-depth forensic analysis, counterparty lookbacks, and SAR recommendations.
3. Tier 3 - Compliance Management & MLRO Approval: Handled by the Chief AML Officer / MLRO. Holds sole authority to approve SAR filings, direct account freezing, or authorize law enforcement disclosures.
4. Tier 4 - Executive AML Oversight Committee: Receives quarterly filings, material regulatory exposure reports, and approves permanent customer de-risking for enterprise clients.

Section 3: Priority Escalation Categories & Response SLAs
- Critical Priority 1 (4-Hour Response SLA):
  * Positive, confirmed matches against OFAC Sanctions, UN, or EU consolidated lists.
  * Direct nexus to terrorism financing or proliferations networks.
  * Credible red flags indicating child exploitation or human trafficking proceeds.
  Action: Immediate freeze of associated transaction and real-time verbal and written briefing to the MLRO.
- High Priority 2 (24-Hour Response SLA):
  * Confirmed structuring schemes aggregating in excess of $100,000 within a 30-day window.
  * Law Enforcement Grand Jury subpoenas or FinCEN 314(a) subject hits.
  * Material bribery or corruption schemes involving senior foreign political figures.
  Action: Immediate escalation from L1 to Senior FIU Investigator with priority case queuing.
- Medium Priority 3 (72-Hour Response SLA):
  * Persistent lack of customer response to multiple Requests for Information (RFI).
  * Recurring uncharacteristic transaction spikes without adequate commercial documentation.

Section 4: Protocol for Transaction Holds and Account Freezing
Administrative holds may only be applied under the explicit written authorization of the MLRO or designated Legal Counsel under any of the following conditions:
1. Valid OFAC sanctions block requirement.
2. Formal court-ordered restraining order or criminal asset forfeiture warrant.
3. Imminent flight risk involving active cybercrime or fraudulent unauthorized diversion of depositor funds exceeding $50,000.

Section 5: USA PATRIOT Act Section 314(b) Information Sharing
To enhance cross-institutional intelligence against illicit networks, Senior Investigators may initiate voluntary 314(b) requests with participating peer financial institutions. All 314(b) transmissions must be encrypted and logged in the FIU audit database.
